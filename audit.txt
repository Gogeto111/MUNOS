OVERVIEW:
The MUNOS application is a comprehensive Model United Nations platform featuring:
- AI-powered committee simulations with real Gemini AI responses
- Delegate portfolio and passport system
- Social network for MUN delegates
- Conference discovery and registration system
- Research assistant with AI-powered briefings
- Analytics dashboard with performance tracking
- Resource library with UN documents and guides
- Organizer tools for conference management
- Video coach for speech analysis
- And much more...
